## The Partnership

Since 2018, Bitcraft has been working with **Text SA** (formerly LiveChat, Inc.) under a B2B contract — a single engineer embedded directly within their product engineering team, working alongside other engineers in a similar arrangement.

This 8+ year continuous engagement is one of Bitcraft's longest-standing partnerships, demonstrating sustained value delivery across multiple product evolutions.

## Products

### livechat.com

The engagement started with **LiveChat** — a leading customer service platform that enables businesses to communicate with their customers in real time. LiveChat serves thousands of businesses worldwide, handling millions of conversations.

### text.com

Currently, the focus is on **Text** — a next-generation application for customer service and sales that leverages chat communication. Text represents the evolution of the LiveChat ecosystem into a broader, multiplatform communication framework supporting web, desktop, and mobile clients.

## Engagement Model

- **Type:** B2B contract — embedded engineer
- **Duration:** 8+ consecutive years (2018 – present)
- **Domain:** Real-time communication, customer service tooling
- **Approach:** Deep integration within the client's engineering team, contributing to product development as a full team member
